# Product Overview

**修行码农** (vc-blog) is a personal blog platform built for sharing technical articles and insights.

## Core Features

- **Article Management**: Full-featured blog with article creation, editing, and publishing
- **Content Organization**: Category-based article organization and browsing
- **Admin Dashboard**: Backend management system for content administration
- **SEO Optimized**: Built-in sitemap, RSS feeds, and metadata management
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first design supporting all device sizes

## Target Audience

Technical professionals and developers interested in software development, architecture, and programming insights.

## Key Pages

- Homepage: Latest articles with pagination
- Article detail pages with date-based URLs (`/article/YYYY/MM/DD/title`)
- Category listing and category-specific pages
- Admin dashboard for content management
- RSS/XML feeds for syndication
